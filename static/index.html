FAQ

How do I download posts from an Instagram profile?

Type the profile's @username in the search bar above. Its public posts load into a grid — tick what you want and hit Download. A profile link works too.

Can I download with just a post link?

Yes. Paste any public post, reel or carousel link and it resolves to the same grid. You can paste several links at once to build a bulk list.

What quality are the downloads?

The best quality Instagram serves for that post — photos as JPG, videos as MP4 with their original audio.

Do I need to log in to Instagram?

Never. FetchGram doesn't ask for your Instagram credentials and never touches your account.

Can I download private profiles?

No. Only public profiles and posts work. Private content is never served publicly by Instagram, so no tool can fetch it.

Is it legal to download Instagram posts?

The content belongs to whoever created it. Only download content you own or have permission to use — downloading doesn't give you rights in it, and reposting or commercial use without permission can infringe copyright.

Why did a download fail?

Usually the post was deleted, the account went private, or Instagram is temporarily rate-limiting requests. Wait a few minutes and try again.
